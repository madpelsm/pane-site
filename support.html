Pane Support

Your Mac, on your iPhone and iPad.

Get help

Ask a question or report a problem by opening an issue on GitHub (a free GitHub account is needed). Issues are public: never include passwords, addresses of your Macs or other personal details. It helps to mention your iPhone or iPad model, your Mac model and its macOS version.

Set up your Mac

  1. On the Mac, open System Settings → General → Sharing and turn on Screen Sharing (or Remote Management).
  2. Under the info button next to it, allow your user account (or "All users").
  3. On your iPhone or iPad, open Pane. Macs on the same network appear on their own; tap one and sign in with that Mac's user name and password. You can also add a Mac by its host name or IP address.

Nothing needs to be installed on the Mac.

High Performance mode

On a Mac with Apple silicon running macOS Sonoma or later, Pane can use the Mac's High Performance mode for a sharper, smoother picture. It works on your local network or a private network such as a VPN. A Mac allows one High Performance session at a time; if another device holds it, Pane uses the standard mode.

From outside your home or office

Pane connects directly to your Mac. To reach it from elsewhere, put both devices on the same private network, for example with a VPN such as Tailscale, and add the Mac by its address on that network.

Using Pane

  • Tap the small handle at the top of the screen for the controls: displays, zoom, clipboard, keyboard, touch mode, shortcuts and disconnect.
  • Pinch to zoom, drag with two fingers to scroll, and swipe with three fingers to move between spaces.
  • On iPad, a hardware keyboard, trackpad or mouse works as it would on the Mac, including Command shortcuts.

Something is not working

  • The Mac does not appear. Check that sharing is on and that both devices are on the same network, and that Pane may use the local network (Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network).
  • Incorrect name or password. Use the Mac user's account name and password, and check that this user is allowed under Sharing.
  • A session problem. Pane keeps a short log of recent sessions in the Files app (On My iPhone or On My iPad → Pane → Diagnostics). It holds no passwords or screen content, but it does show your Mac's network address: remove that line before you attach it to an issue.
