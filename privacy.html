Privacy Policy

Pane for iPhone and iPad · Effective 25 November 2026

Pane does not collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Pane has no server: nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • Your Macs. The names and addresses of the Macs you connect to are saved in the app on your device.
  • Passwords. When you connect to a Mac, its user name and password are saved in your device's Keychain, on this device only. They are not synced and are only ever sent to that Mac, when you connect.
  • Diagnostics. Pane may keep a short technical log of recent sessions (device and app version, the Mac's network address, connection steps and stream statistics; never passwords, keys or screen content). It stays on your device, visible in the Files app, and is only shared if you share it yourself.

Like other app data, the list of Macs and the logs are part of your device backups.

Network connections

Pane connects only to the Macs you choose, directly over your network (or a private network you set up, such as a VPN). To find nearby Macs, Pane looks for them on your local network; iOS asks for your permission first. Your screen, keyboard and pointer input travel only between your device and your Mac.

Deleting your data

Remove a Mac from the list in Pane to delete its saved details and its password. Deleting the app removes the list of Macs and the logs; iOS may keep saved passwords in the Keychain after an app is deleted, so remove your Macs in Pane first if you want those gone too.

Children

Pane collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Pane is made by MADEP in Belgium. Questions about privacy can be asked through the support page on GitHub; please do not post passwords or other personal details there.
